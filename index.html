The eight pillars

Everything the best platforms (Whova, Swapcard, Brella, Grip) do well — reimagined African-first and wrapped in one brand.

🌍

Multi-event hub

Every OAF event in one app, one profile, one growing network. Your contacts never reset.

🗓️

Smart program

Multi-track agenda, personal schedule, reminders, calendar sync, live Q&A & polls.

🤝

AI matchmaking

Intent-based matches with a score & reason; improves with behaviour. Quality, not spam.

📅

Meeting booking

Book 1:1s from shared availability, auto speed-networking, real-time conflict handling.

💬

Chat & community

1:1, groups, organiser broadcasts and topic circles — preserved after the event.

⭐

Sponsor engine

Tiered booths, QR lead capture, sponsored circles, CRM export & live ROI dashboard.

🏆

Gamification

Points, badges, challenges & leaderboards — sponsor-brandable to drive real interactions.

📶

African-first & offline

PWA, low-data mode, WhatsApp/SMS fallback, multilingual EN/FR/PT/AR.
