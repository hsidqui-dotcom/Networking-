The eight pillars

Everything the best platforms (Whova, Swapcard, Brella, Grip) do well — reimagined African-first, in OneAfrica's brand.

🌍

Multi-event hub

Land on a page with every OneAfrica event. One profile, one growing network across them all.

🗓️

Smart program

Multi-track agenda, session details, personal schedule, reminders & calendar sync.

🤝

AI matchmaking

Intent-based matches with a score & reason; improves with behaviour. Quality, not spam.

📅

Meeting booking

Book 1:1s from shared availability, auto speed-networking, real-time conflict handling.

💬

Chat & community

1:1, groups, organiser broadcasts and topic circles — preserved after the event.

⭐

Sponsor engine

Tiered booths, QR lead capture, sponsored circles, CRM export & live ROI dashboard.

🎤

Speakers & content

Speaker profiles, bios, sessions, slides & replays — discoverable long after.

📶

African-first & offline

PWA, low-data mode, WhatsApp/SMS fallback, multilingual EN/FR/PT/AR.
